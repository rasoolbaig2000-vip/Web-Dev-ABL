📖 Library Rules

  • Students may borrow up to three books at a time.
  • The standard borrowing period is 14 days.
  • Return books on or before the due date.
  • Keep books clean and avoid writing on their pages.
  • Maintain silence inside the library.
  • Report lost or damaged books to the librarian.
  • Return books to the designated counter.

Note: These are sample library rules and can be customized.
